How It Works

The path a job posting takes through JobGuard's assessment flow
1
Job Input
You paste a job title and description, with optional recruiter email and job URL for extra signal.
2
AI Orchestrator
The posting is routed through JobGuard's assessment pipeline, coordinating each analysis component below.
3
Analysis Components
Multiple checks run in parallel:
  • Rule-based risk indicators
  • AI context analysis
  • Email / URL verification
4
Controlled Risk Scoring
Findings are weighted into a single risk score and confidence level, with a full breakdown by category.
5
AI Explanation & Safety Recommendations
JobGuard explains what it found in plain language and recommends next steps to stay safe.
Important Disclaimer

JobGuard provides an AI-assisted risk assessment based on available evidence. It does not guarantee that a job posting is legitimate or fraudulent.
